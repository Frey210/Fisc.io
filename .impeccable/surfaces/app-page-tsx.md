## Direction contract
THESIS: A conversational finance desk: each sample message visibly becomes a ledger entry. Product demonstration earns attention.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper, dark forest ink, pale mint stage and lime actions; Manrope typography, generous open composition, ledger rules rather than glass cards.

STORY: Understand text-to-transaction, explore a sample, then create an account and link Telegram. Keep real routes and PWA installation.

FORM: User-approved editorial direction; no random seed because the user pinned the world. Code-first. Signature interaction: sample selection updates chat and ledger on a rotating, explodable device assembly. One slow idle motion; pause and reduced-motion supported.
